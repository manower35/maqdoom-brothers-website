A Golden History Since 1895

Historic Clothiers to the Nizams of Hyderabad

For more than a century, **Maqdoom Bros Designers Pvt Ltd** has occupied pride of place along the famed arches of Pathergatti, directly opposite the historic Madina Building. Having historically served as royal clothiers to the Nizams of Hyderabad, our house is credited as the undisputed pioneer of the authentic Hyderabadi sherwani.

Passed down through four generations of family craftsmanship, every garment embodies the regal spirit of the Asaf Jahi court. We seamlessly blend centuries-old hand-zardozi and dabka embroidery with refined bespoke tailoring that fits like a second skin.

The Art of Made-to-Measure

The 3-Step Bespoke Process

🪡

Every royal outfit is customized to your posture, height, skin tone, and wedding theme. Our master tailors ensure an exquisite fit that stands tall for generations in your wedding portraits.

01

Style & Silhouette Consultation

Whether in-store at Pathergatti or via high-definition video call, we examine your event timings, venue lighting, and partner's bridal attire to formulate the ideal color palette and silhouette.

02

Fabric Curation & Hand Embroidery

Choose from authentic Banarasi katan silks, hand-loomed raw silks, and imported Italian wools. Our master Old City zardozi artisans begin the painstaking hand embroidery, beadwork, and mukaish detailing.

03

Precision Fitting & Final Delivery

Experience our signature double trial process to sculpt every seam to your physique. For outstation & international clients, we coordinate millimeter-perfect measurements and ship globally.
